Plugin Presets:


After installing, you have the option of using Jabo's Video/Audio/Input plugin code by default with Project64.exe, or modern and up-to-date options for modern ROMhacks and modern PC's with Project64_Modern.exe. Just bear in mind that the default modern plugins consume more system resources and do not perform as well as the classic Jabo plugins and are supported by less PCs and for these reasons aren't suggested by me for several notable classic games like Super Mario 64, Mario Kart 64, and Ocarina of Time despite the minor improvements. However, the modern plugins preset does support more games overall than the classic Jabo plugins thanks to said minor improvements.
